# Security policy

Reclama is a portfolio project, not a hosted service with real customers. Its demo holds only fictitious data. Reports are still welcome.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not describe a vulnerability in a public issue.

Use GitHub's private reporting: **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**. It stays private between you and the maintainer.

You can expect a first answer within a week. There is no bounty.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository and the demo at <https://reclama-app.netlify.app>.

Out of scope: denial of service by volume, the free-tier limits of Netlify, Neon and Resend, and social engineering.

## What is already known

The known limitations are listed in the [README](README.md#known-limitations) and in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#10-known-limitations-and-trade-offs). Please check them before reporting.
